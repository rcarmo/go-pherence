# Nemotron PTX cleanup — CPU-only defensive correction, 30 September 2026

The PTX diarization tower now reports failed CUDA synchronisation and frees, keeps unconfirmed device owners reachable, and rejects subsequent work after an uncertain outcome. This corrects the error/ownership paths identified in the [source audit](nemotron-gpu-source-safety-audit-20260930.md). The incident binary `ede9ea019fad6732962c9dddc49578fc1add9c90` selected Vulkan when the fence failure surfaced; the corrections do not establish the origin of Xid 79.

## Changed contract

- `Buffer.FreeChecked()` sets `Ptr=0` and increments free statistics only after successful context selection and `cuMemFree`. The legacy `Free()` still has no error return; the PTX owners in this change use the checked form. `SyncErr()` no longer certifies completion after a failed context selection.
- `PTXAudioTower.Close()` waits for a successful sync before releasing scratch, final norm buffers or layers. Layer close also syncs; the standalone layer serialises calls and close through host download and deferred cleanup. Scratch construction checks sync before rollback. A failed sync/free seals the owner, retains all unconfirmed allocations and returns the same error on repeated close. Successful frees are removed from owner lists so a later failed free does not double-free them.
- Tower forward and standalone layer transient cleanup sync before freeing. Failed sync/free retains the transient owners and rejects subsequent use. Partially constructed layers, scratch and towers remain reachable when rollback cannot confirm cleanup. `PTXStreamingTower` and `PCMStreamingRequest.ClosePTXTower()` retain a failed tower; the CLI already joins the close error into its result.
- PTX stacking projector close syncs before freeing its buffers, reports and retains failed cleanup, and seals the owner after a failed projection upload, launch, sync or download. A failed PTX upload/launch/sync/download retains owners without blind cleanup. Vulkan projector close now memoises a failed arena/operator close and retains failed and unvisited owners; repeated close returns the same error without retry.

A retained CUDA pointer is diagnostic ownership, not permission to retry a free or run another request after device/context loss. The terminal owner deliberately retains memory until a separate process-level recovery decision. Other legacy `Free()` callers and CUDA entry points were not converted in this bounded pass.
