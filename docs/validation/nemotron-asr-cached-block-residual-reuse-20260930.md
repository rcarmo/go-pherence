# Nemotron ASR cached-block residual reuse — 30 September 2026

`Encoder0Block.ForwardCachedChunk` now adds FF1's residual into the owned cached-attention output buffer. That output had been discarded after allocating a separate residual slice. The convolution consumes the sum without storing or returning it, while its output and the final FF2 output remain owned. Any failed downstream stage still leaves both caller caches uncommitted. No tolerance or GPU implementation changed.

Baseline: clean `2bacf16bdb786458d0b1c969d8f1e7888547696d`, Go 1.26.3, Linux amd64, i7-12700, `GOMAXPROCS=4`, released ASR checkpoint SHA-256 `9eebdd6590289cb3030f310858f3df93256600a800a3e8200c5993d5f967e174`. Three three-iteration runs of `BenchmarkPCMGenerationStreamJFK` after the change used **1,299,276,736 B/op and 23,964 allocs/op**, compared with five earlier five-iteration samples at **1,305,974,515 B/op and 24,756 allocs/op**. This saves about **6.70 MB and 792 allocations** per 11-second warm request, on top of the [earlier cached-attention reuse](nemotron-asr-cached-attention-buffer-reuse-20260930.md). Timings after the change were 6.47–6.68 seconds per request; before were 6.24–6.61 seconds. These ranges overlap, and the iteration counts differ. No speedup is accepted from this comparison.
